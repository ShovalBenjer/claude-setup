# Retrieval and vector workflows

Version the corpus, chunker, embedding model, index, and evaluation set. Measure
retrieval and answer quality separately with representative queries. Preserve
source attribution and deletion paths. Do not embed secrets or raw PII, and do not
treat plausible generated text as evidence that retrieval was correct.

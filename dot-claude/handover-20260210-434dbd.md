### FIX 1: Axia Prompt — Adaptive Escalation Empathy (recovers 5 tests)

**Root Cause**: Axia uses **identical** escalation template for ALL scenarios (lines 804-860 of `agent-prompts/axia-cs-system-prompt-v28.md`). Same "I understand your concern. I'm creating a support ticket..." for cakes, fraud, and emotional distress.

**Affected Tests**:
| Test ID | Scenario | What's Wrong |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| ESC-MEDIUM-02 | Off-topic (cake question) | Creates ticket instead of polite redirect |
| ESC-MEDIUM-04 | Customer in distress | No empathy, jumps to ticket template |
| ESC-HARD-03 | Fraud accusation + FSA threat | Same generic template, no urgency acknowledgment |
| SCENARIO-05-axia | Restricted account | No KYC explanation, just ticket |
| AXIA-ML-TYPO-EN-01 | Account lockout with typos | Generic response, no lockout-specific help |

**Exact Fix**: Replace the single template block (lines 804-860) with severity-tiered responses. Add these sections BEFORE the templates:

```markdown
## ESCALATION TIERS (MANDATORY — match tier to situation)

### Tier 0: OFF-TOPIC → Polite Redirect (NO escalation, NO ticket)
When the query is about restaurants, weather, sports, recipes, etc.:
- "I appreciate the question! I'm specifically trained to help with Axia trading account matters. Is there anything related to your account I can assist with?"
- NEVER create a ticket for off-topic queries
- NEVER use the word "escalate" for non-financial questions

### Tier 1: SIMPLE HELP → Standard Ticket
When the query is a normal support request (password reset, document upload, withdrawal timing):
- "I understand. Let me create a support ticket so our team can assist you directly."
- Professional, efficient, no excess empathy

### Tier 2: COMPLAINT → Empathize + Acknowledge + Escalate with Urgency
When the customer is frustrated or complaining about service:
- FIRST acknowledge their specific frustration: "I can see this has been a frustrating experience, especially waiting [X days] for [specific issue]."
- THEN explain what happens next: "I'm escalating this to our senior support team as a priority case."
- Include specific timeframe commitments

### Tier 3: LEGAL/FRAUD/REGULATORY → Strong Apology + Immediate Senior Escalation
When the customer mentions fraud, lawyers, regulators, FSA, scam:
- OPEN with strong acknowledgment: "I take this very seriously. Allegations of [fraud/unauthorized activity] require immediate attention from our compliance team."
- DO NOT minimize or use generic language
- Mention the specific concern they raised
- Commit to senior/compliance team escalation

### Tier 4: EMOTIONAL DISTRESS → Extended Empathy Before ANY Procedure
When the customer shows signs of distress (ALL CAPS, "ruined my life", crying, desperate):
- Spend 2-3 sentences on EMPATHY before any procedural steps
- "I hear you, and I want you to know that what you're feeling is completely valid."
- "Let me make sure the right person helps you through this."
- NEVER jump straight to "provide your login ID" with a distressed customer
```

**Where to edit**: `agent-prompts/axia-cs-system-prompt-v28.md` lines 804-860. Keep the localized templates but add a preamble: "Choose the appropriate tier FIRST, then use the matching template style."

### FIX 3: Test Framework — Double-Eval Bug (reduces flakiness, may recover borderline tests)

**Root Cause**: `tests/deepeval_suite.py` line 258 calls `assert_test(tc, [tn])` which re-evaluates the tone metric with the LLM judge. But `_measure_metric()` (line 248) already evaluated it. With `temperature=1`, the two evaluations can give different scores.

**Impact**: Results JSON sometimes shows PASS but pytest marks FAILED (or vice versa). 3 borderline tests at 0.6 score may flip on any run.

**Exact Fix**: In `tests/deepeval_suite.py`, replace line 258:
```python
# BEFORE (line 258):
assert_test(tc, [tn])

# AFTER:
assert tn.is_successful(), f"Tone failed: score={tn.score}, reason={tn.reason}"
```

Also do the same for any other `assert_test()` calls in the file — each one should be replaced with manual assertion from the already-measured metric.

## Key Decisions & Learnings

### Prescriptive Criteria Pattern (BREAKTHROUGH)
Instead of telling the GEval judge what's NOT a failure (descriptive), give it exactly 3 patterns that score 0.0 and say everything else is 1.0. The judge's reasoning engine overrides descriptive exceptions but follows prescriptive checklists.

### FIRST RULE Pattern
Place critical exceptions (off-topic redirect = not hallucination) BEFORE pattern checks. Judge follows first-seen rules more reliably than embedded NOTEs within patterns.

### Tone Rubric Isolation
Tone judge must be explicitly told "Do NOT evaluate factual accuracy." Without this, gpt-5.2 evaluates whether fee amounts are "real" within tone scoring — cross-contamination.

### Judge Variance
gpt-5.2 at temperature=1 has ~2-3% stochastic variance. Same test can pass then fail. Binary scoring + prescriptive criteria minimizes but doesn't eliminate. Accept as structural noise.

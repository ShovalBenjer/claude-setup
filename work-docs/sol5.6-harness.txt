A challenge I’d give you
If your goal is to build a truly next-generation Claude Code harness by 2027–2030, I’d frame the project around one question:
Can you make the harness measurably smarter every week without upgrading the underlying model?
If the answer is yes, you’re no longer just wrapping Claude—you’ve built a learning AI runtime.
That’s the direction I expect the frontier to move: models will keep improving, but the biggest competitive advantage will increasingly come from the surrounding system—evaluation, orchestration, memory, verification, optimization, and continuous self-improvement. Those are all engineering problems as much as they are AI problems.

name: sales-account-research
description: "Load when the user asks to research a company, account, or prospect before sales outreach, a sales call, or a customer meeting — e.g. 'research Acme Corp before a sales call', 'find the key buyer contacts at this account', 'give me pre-call sales intel on this company'. Do not load for recruiting outreach, partnerships/BD, fundraising or investor research, journalism, real-time stock prices, broad competitive landscape analysis, or individual consumer background checks."

Sales Account Research
Scope
In: Company profile, firmographics, recent news, hiring signals, key people, tech stack, CRM history, qualification signals.

Out: Real-time financial/stock data, competitive market analysis, consumer/individual background checks. For post-research outreach, see draft-outreach. For call prep structure, see call-prep.

Research Checklist
Run these in order. Prioritize enrichment connector data over web search when both are available — enrichment sources are more precise.

Resolve the target — if given a company name, confirm the correct domain (e.g. acme.com) to avoid ambiguity on common names

Enrichment connector (if connected) — pull verified contacts, tech stack, org chart, funding details

CRM connector (if connected) — pull prior relationship history, past opportunities, existing contacts; flag stale contacts (> 12 months no activity)

Web and people search — company overview, funding stage, recent news (last 90 days), leadership changes, product launches, open roles, key contacts

Synthesize — map findings to the output sections below

No connectors available? Fall back to web search + LinkedIn or another public people-search source for contacts. Note data confidence is lower; flag this in the output.

Connector Reference
Connector	What It Adds
Enrichment (e.g. Apollo, Clay, ZoomInfo)	Verified emails, phone, tech stack, org chart, funding details
CRM (e.g. Salesforce, HubSpot)	Prior relationship history, past opportunities, existing contacts
Output Sections
Quick Take — 2-3 sentences: who they are, why they might need you, best outreach angle

Company Profile — firmographic table (industry, size, HQ, funding stage, tech stack highlights)

Key People — per-person card: name, title, tenure, background, talking point

Qualification Signals — positive indicators / concerns / unknowns for discovery

Recommended Approach — best entry point, opening hook, 2-3 discovery questions

Gotchas
Enrich before searching — enrichment connector data is more accurate than web; always check it first if available.

Domain lookup beats name lookup — "research acme.com" avoids false matches on common company names; always confirm the domain before pulling data.

Enrichment data lags — employee counts and org charts can be 3-6 months stale; cross-reference open roles on LinkedIn or the company careers page.

CRM contacts go stale — people change roles frequently; verify titles before any outreach.

Hiring velocity > headcount — 20 open ML engineering roles is a stronger buying signal than raw employee count; always surface role-mix and growth direction.

Research ≠ qualification — this skill surfaces facts; use discovery calls to validate fit assumptions. Do not assert qualification in the output.

Partial data is still useful — if enrichment or CRM is missing, produce the brief with available data and explicitly mark gaps rather than refusing or asking the user to reconnect.
